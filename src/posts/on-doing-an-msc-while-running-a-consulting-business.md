## Why I'm doing this

The MSc in Data Science at Strathmore is not a career pivot. It's a foundation reinforcement. Ten years of applied work has given me strong intuitions about what works — the degree is about understanding _why_ it works at a level that makes me a better researcher, not just a better practitioner.

The PhD in Applied Statistics is the long game. This is the next step on that path.

## The logistics problem

Running Anselmelly Consulting while studying full time is a scheduling problem, not a motivation problem. I know I want to do both. The question is how to structure the time so neither suffers.

What I'm putting in place before May:

  * **Client load reduction.** I'm not taking new retainer clients until September. Existing ones stay. New project work is case by case.
  * **Async-first communication.** I've moved most client communication to written async channels. Fewer calls, better documentation, same outcomes.
  * **Protected study blocks.** Tuesday and Thursday mornings are study time. Non-negotiable. Clients know this.



## What I'm not doing

I'm not pretending I'll be equally productive on all fronts. Something will give on some weeks. The goal is to make sure it's never the same thing two weeks in a row — that's the early warning sign of a system that's failing.
